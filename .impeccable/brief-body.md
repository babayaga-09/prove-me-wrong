# Thesis check (home route)

Scope: the thesis check, Operate mode. Audience: an Indian retail investor (with a recruiter as secondary audience). Job: pick a Nifty 50 stock, write or load a three-sentence thesis, test it, and read a per-sentence verdict with evidence and track records. Success: a demo thesis tested in 30 seconds with no sign-up. Must read as a serious, premium finance product: calm, uncluttered, no novelty type.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your thesis comes back as a private-bank statement on your own view. Each sentence is a line item with its evidence, a verdict and a balance bar showing which way the evidence leans and how strongly. A double-ruled total line closes the statement. It refuses the fintech default of a ticker header, green/red tiles and card grids.

OWN-WORLD: White ground (#FFFFFF); the scene is daytime on a laptop. A full-height deep navy shell rail (#0F1E3D) carries the wordmark and nav in white. Navy ink (#0F1E3D) for text, slate (#4B5563) for secondary text, hairlines #E5E7EB. One brass accent (#B08D57) is reserved for selection, focus and the balance marker. Semantic tints: For #1F7A5C, Against #B3261E, Open #6B7280, each always paired with an icon and a word. Type: Source Serif 4 for the company name and section titles only; Geist for all UI; tabular numerals everywhere. Statement tables: hairline rows, right-aligned figures, no boxes around rows, no shadows, no texture.

STORY: The visitor lands on a loaded example (HDFC Bank, three sentences) and presses Test my thesis. The rows resolve top to bottom: verdict chip, balance bar and a restated line where the evidence disagrees. The evidence panel explains the selected row with each model's track record. The "What would prove me wrong" tab lists a tripwire per sentence. Then they edit a sentence or pick another stock.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: a navy rail (PMW wordmark, Nifty 50 search, Thesis check, Scoreboard, How it works; data date at the foot). Header: the company name in serif, with sector · last close · date beneath; the stock select and the primary navy "Test my thesis" button on the right; example theses as quiet text links. Main: the statement table (# | Your sentence | Evidence | Lean | Verdict) with tabs "Your thesis" / "What would prove me wrong", and an evidence panel on the right for the selected row. Below: a "Signals" statement table (signal | reading | model record).

FORM: Private Client Report, my grounded conventional candidate 1 of 3 in a safer-register re-roll (round 1) of seed f9e177e0. It replaces the rejected Mixtape J-card build.

Signature interaction: the Lean column. A diverging balance bar per sentence (Against ← | → For), its length set by the verdict's confidence (measured > tested > weak > untested), with a brass tick at the net position. On Test, the rows resolve in sequence and the bars grow from the centre line. The total row sits under an accounting double rule. Under reduced motion, everything resolves at once.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
